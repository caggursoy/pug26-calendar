# PuG 2026 Calendar

A small Python utility that scrapes the PUG 2026 conference agenda and writes the sessions into Google Calendar.

---

Click the following Google Calendar link to add your instance.

### Create your own personalised copy

If you want to remove sessions you won't attend, follow the steps below to duplicate the calendar and edit your own copy.

#### Step 1: Export the Calendar

1. Go to [this link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1nKYj1t0bnFOkBodkQ4t3s9GmghEe80U-/view?usp=sharing) and download the `.ics` calendar file.

#### Step 2: Create a New Calendar

1. Go to [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2. Click the `+` icon next to **Other calendars** in the left sidebar.
3. Select **Create new calendar**.
4. Fill in the name and click **Create calendar**.

#### Step 3: Import the `.ics` File

1. Click the gear icon in the top-right corner and select **Settings**.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Import & export**.
3. Under **Import**, click **Select file from your computer** and choose the `.ics` file you downloaded.
4. In the **Add to calendar** dropdown, select the new calendar you just created.
5. Click **Import**.

Your new calendar now contains all PuG 2026 sessions. Uncheck the original calendar from the **My calendars** panel and start editing your own copy.
